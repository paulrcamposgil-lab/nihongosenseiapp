The book

What is Minna no Nihongo?

Minna no Nihongo (“Japanese for everyone”) is the Japanese textbook used by many language schools around the world. It starts from zero and moves in short lessons: each one brings its vocabulary, one or two grammar points and sentences to use them.

The beginner level is two books and 50 lessons: 1 to 37 in the first, 26 to 50 in the second. Finishing them takes you roughly to level N4 of the JLPT, the best-known Japanese exam. The intermediate books come after.

Nihongo Sensei follows that order lesson by lesson: each lesson’s vocabulary with audio, its grammar explained with our own examples, and exercises where you type the answer. Today it reaches lesson 37; lessons 38 to 50 are on the way.

When you get it wrong

It doesn’t say “incorrect”. It names the rule you used.

A red cross leaves you exactly where you were. Knowing you treated a Group I verb as if it were Group II leaves you with a rule — and with the other words that follow it.

And there’s a screen that tracks which rules you’re weak at, not which words you missed. That’s the thing you can actually fix.

  • Group II · drop ますnearly there
