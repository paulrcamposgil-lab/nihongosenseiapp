Minna no Nihongo lesson 1: vocabulary & grammar

Vocabulary

39 words from lesson 1, with their reading in kana and rōmaji, and an example sentence under each.

JapaneseMeaningListen
わたしwatashiI
わたしはニコです。Watashi wa niko desu.I am Nico.
あなたanataYou
あなたはがくせいですか。Anata wa gakusei desu ka.Are you a student?
あのひとano hitoThat person, he, she (あの　かた is the polite equivalent)
あのひとはだれですか。Ano hito wa dare desu ka.Who is that person?
さんsanMr., Ms. (suffix added to a name for expressing politeness)
やまださんはせんせいです。Yamada-san wa sensei desu.Mr. Yamada is a teacher.
ちゃんchan(suffix often added to a child's name instead of ~san)
テレサちゃんはきゅうさいです。Teresa chan wa kyuu sai desu.Teresa-chan (the suffix for a child) is nine years old.
じんjinSuffix indicating a person's nationality.
わたしはスペインじんです。Watashi wa supein jin desu.I am a person from Spain: Spanish.
せんせい先生senseiTeacher, instructor, doctor, respectful title. (Not used when referring to one's own profession. In this case 教師, きょうし, is preferred.)
やまださんはにほんごのせんせいです。Yamada-san wa nihongo no sensei desu.Mr. Yamada is a Japanese teacher.

はじめましてhajimemashiteNice to meet you, opening greeting (said FIRST when meeting someone; ≠ どうぞよろしくおねがいします from L1, which closes the introduction)

Grammar

The patterns of lesson 1 and their examples, as the app explains them.

Nは Nです — Identity

  • N1は N2 ですAffirmative
  • N1は N2 じゃありませんNegative
  • N1は N2 ですかQuestion
  • N1も N2 ですAlso / too
  • N1の N2Possessive

Examples

  • わたしは ノアです。watashi wa Noa desu.I am Noa.
  • アナさんも がくせいです。Ana-san mo gakusei desu.Ana is also a student.
  • だいがくの せんせいです。daigaku no sensei desu.They are a university professor.
