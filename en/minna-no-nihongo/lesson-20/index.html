Minna no Nihongo lesson 20: vocabulary & grammar

Vocabulary

22 words from lesson 20, with their reading in kana and rōmaji, and an example sentence under each.

JapaneseMeaningListen
いりますirimasuTo need, be necessary
おかねがいります。Okane ga irimasu.You need money.
しらべます調べますshirabemasuTo check, to look up
じしょでしらべます。Jisho de shirabemasu.I look it up in the dictionary.
しゅうりします修理しますshuuri shimasuto repair machines/appliances
くるまをしゅうりします。Kuruma o shuuri shimasu.I repair the car.
ぼくbokuI, casual masculine
ぼくはがくせいです。Boku wa gakusei desu.I am a student.
きみkimiYou, casual
きみも いく？Kimi mo iku?Are you going too?
くん~kunMr. (an informal equivalent of 〜さん used to address people of equal or lower status; also often appended to boys' names)
たろうくんは げんき？Tarou kun wa genki?How is young Taro doing?
うんunYes, casual
あしたいく？うん、いくよ。Ashita iku? un, iku yo.Going tomorrow? — Yeah, I'm going.
ううんuunNo, casual
もうたべた？ううん、まだ。Mou tabeta? uun, mada.Have you eaten? — Nope, not yet.
ことば言葉kotobaword, language
にほんごのことばをおぼえます。Nihongo no kotoba o oboemasu.I learn words in Japanese.
きもの着物kimonokimono (traditional Japanese attire)
おしょうがつにきものをきます。Oshougatsu ni kimono o kimasu.On New Year's I wear the kimono.
ビザbizavisa
ビザをとります。Biza o torimasu.I get the visa.
はじめ初めhajimethe beginning
はじめのうちはむずかしかったです。Hajime no uchi wa muzukashikatta desu.In the beginning it was difficult.
おわりowarithe end of ~, The End
しごとがおわりました。Shigoto ga owarimashita.The work has come to an end.
こっちkocchiOver here, casual
こっちへ きて。Kocchi e kite.Come over here.
そっちsocchiOver there, near you, casual
そっちは あつい？Socchi wa atsui?Is it hot over there?
あっちacchiOver there, casual
トイレはあっちだよ。Toire wa acchi da yo.The bathroom is over there.
どっちdocchiWhich of the two, casual
コーヒーと こうちゃ、どっちが いい？Koohii to koucha, docchi ga ii?Coffee or tea — which do you want?
みんなでminna deAll together, everyone
にちようびに みんなで うみへ いきました。Nichiyoubi ni minna de umi e ikimashita.On Sunday we all went to the sea together.
けどkedoBut, casual
たかいけど、いいものです。Takai kedo ii mono desu.It's expensive, but it's good.
おなかがいっぱいですonaka ga ippai desuFull (stomach), can't eat more
たくさんたべました。おなかが いっぱいです。Takusan tabemashita. onaka ga ippai desu.I ate a lot. I'm full.
よかったらyokattaraif you like
よかったら、いっしょに たべない？Yokattara, issho ni tabenai?If you like, want to eat together?
いろいろiroiroVarious, all sorts (without な; = the same word, which goes before a noun in L10)
いろいろなものをたべました。Iroiro na mono o tabemashita.I ate various things.

Grammar

The patterns of lesson 20 and their examples, as the app explains them.

Casual Speech

  • V[dict] (たべる、いく…)Present positive
  • V-ない (たべない、いかない…)Present negative
  • V-た (たべた、いった…)Past positive
  • V-なかった (たべなかった…)Past negative
  • ～？ (without か)Informal question
  • [い] (さむい、たかい…)i-adj informal
  • [な] だ (しずかだ…)na-adj informal

Examples

  • きょう くる？kyou kuru?Are you coming today?
  • スーパーへ いかない。suupaa e ikanai.I am not going to the supermarket.
  • きょうは さむい。kyou wa samui.It is cold today.
