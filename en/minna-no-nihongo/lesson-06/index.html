Minna no Nihongo lesson 6: vocabulary & grammar

Vocabulary

50 words from lesson 6, with their reading in kana and rōmaji, and an example sentence under each.

たべます食べますtabemasuTo eat [something] (≠ to have a meal, from L13)
まいにちやさいをたべます。Mainichi yasai wo tabemasu.I eat vegetables every day.

まいあさコーヒーをのみます。Maiasa koohii wo nomimasu.I drink coffee every morning.

わたしはタバコをすいません。Watashi wa tabako wo suimasen.I don't smoke.
みます見ますmimasuTo see, look at, watch
まいばんテレビをみます。Maiban terebi wo mimasu.I watch television every night.
ききます聞きますkikimasuTo hear, listen
まいばんラジオをききます。Maiban rajio wo kikimasu.I listen to the radio every night.
よみます読みますyomimasuTo read
このほんをよみます。Kono hon wo yomimasu.I read this book.
かきます書きますkakimasuTo write (can also mean to draw or paint)

Grammar

The patterns of lesson 6 and their examples, as the app explains them.

を — Transitive Verbs

  • N を V-ますDirect object
  • どこで N を V-ますかWhere + what
  • V-ませんかInvitation
  • V-ましょうSuggestion
  • なにも V-ませんNothing

Examples

  • ビールを のみます。biiru wo nomimasu.I drink beer.
  • いっしょに コーヒーを のみませんか。issho ni koohii wo nomimasen ka.Shall we have coffee together?
  • なにも たべませんでした。nani mo tabemasen deshita.I did not eat anything.
