Grammar

The grammar points of lesson 1 and their examples, as the app explains them.

X は Y です — "X is Y"

です is the "equals sign" of Japanese, and it always sits at the very end. Noun + です is already a complete sentence: かいしゃいんです can be "I'm an office worker", "she's an office worker"… The listener fills in who from the situation. When the situation isn't clear, put a label in front: X は. Read it as a headline: "Hana → office worker." That little は is a particle; it's spelled with the kana は but always sounds wa.

  • ケンタさんねんせいです。Sannensei desu.I'm a third-year.
  • ケンタハナさんは かいしゃいんです。Hana san wa kaishain desu.Hana is an office worker.
  • ニコきむらさんは よんじゅうにさいです。Kimura san wa yonjūni sai desu.Mr. Kimura is 42.

がくせいです doesn't say "a" or "the", or whether it's one student or five. Japanese leaves that to context. And さん is for others only: ニコです, never ニコさんです.

Question sentences

To turn a statement into a question, just hang か on the end. No word order changes, no question mark needed. To say "yes, right": はい、そうです. To say no, start with いいえ and give the correct fact. For "what/how many" questions, use なん glued to a counter: なんじ (what time), なんさい (what age), なんねんせい (which year), なんばん (which number). The answer simply puts the real number in that same slot.

  • ハナケンタさんは ほっかいどうの がくせいですか。 — いいえ、みなとだいがくの がくせいです。Kenta, are you a student in Hokkaido? — No, at Minato University.
  • ノアケンタさんは みなとだいがくの なんねんせいですか。 — さんねんせいです。What year is Kenta in at Minato University? — Third year.
  • ケンタニコさんは なにじんですか。 — スペインじんです。What nationality is Nico? — Spanish.

なん or なに? Rule of thumb: if the next sound is d-, t-, n- or a counter, say なん (なんですか, なんじ). Before a particle, なに. Nationality is the odd one: なにじん. The question word sits exactly where the answer will go: ハナさんの ともだちは なにじんですか → ハナさんの ともだちは かんこくじんです.

Noun₁ の Noun₂

Read の as an arrow pointing right: the noun at the end of the chain is the thing you are actually talking about, and everything before の is a sticker on it (whose, from where, about what). Spanish "de" points the other way, so flip the order: "the economics student" → けいざいの がくせい. You can chain more than two, and the whole block behaves like one noun anywhere in the sentence.

  • ノアバルセロナだいがくの がくせいです。I'm a University of Barcelona student.
  • ケンタニコさんの いもうとは ノアさんです。Nico's younger sister is Noa.
  • ハナケンタさんの ともだちの でんわばんごうthe phone number of Kenta's friend

Lost? Say the Spanish backwards: «teléfono de amigo de Kenta» → Kenta の amigo の teléfono.
