Nihongo Sensei — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Nihongo Sensei does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All your progress, preferences, and quiz statistics are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

No tracking

We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.

No accounts

The app does not require account creation, login, or any personal information to use.

Payments

Payment for the app is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@nihongosenseiapp.com.
