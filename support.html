Need help with the app, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Reach us at support@nihongosenseiapp.com — we read every message.

Common questions

How do I switch between Spanish and English?
Open the menu (··· in the top right) → Language.

How do I unlock Lessons 3–25?
These are part of Nihongo Sensei Premium, an annual subscription. You can subscribe from any locked lesson, and manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your device's Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.

I bought Premium but it's not showing up.
Open the menu → and use "Restore Purchases" (or reach out to us directly if you don't see that option yet — it may not be available in your current app version).

Is my study progress backed up anywhere?
Your progress is stored on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
