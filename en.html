Who is Nihongo Sensei for?

For anyone who goes to class with Minna no Nihongo and wants to arrive ready. Every lesson in the book has its vocabulary here, with the reading, an example sentence and its translation, plus the grammar notes that go with it. You open lesson 12 and you practise lesson 12 — nothing to look up.

For anyone studying alone who needs something to say what to review today. The app keeps track of what you get wrong and brings it back when it should, not when it feels like it: words you struggle with come back sooner, words you know get spaced out. And for anyone who quit another app because the streak was stressing them out — there are no streaks here, no leagues, and nothing to lose if you skip a day.

It was built by a student following the same textbook, to study the way he wanted to study. Interface, meanings and explanations in English and in Spanish.
